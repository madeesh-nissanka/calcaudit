# Limitations (read before relying on a report)

1. A clean report means the listed rules passed on the parsed equations, not that the document is correct.
2. The coverage line shows how much could be checked. Low coverage means most equations were not understood.
3. Unit checks need declared units. Wrong declarations give wrong verdicts.
4. Randomised equivalence is strong evidence, not proof.
5. The knowledge cards are generic and unreviewed. Treat card mismatches as questions.
